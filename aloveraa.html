Aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis Miller) is a thick, short-stemmed succulent plant
famously known for its cooling, therapeutic gel. Originating from the Arabian Peninsula,
it thrives in dry, tropical climates and has been utilized for centuries in
traditional systems like Ayurveda. The plant contains over 75
bioactive components, including vitamins (A, C, E, and B12), minerals
(zinc, magnesium, calcium), enzymes, and powerful antioxidants.
